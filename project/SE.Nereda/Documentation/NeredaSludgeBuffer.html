IBlowerReactor
This adapter, 'IDedicatedBlower,' receives start and stop commands through the StartBlower variable, as well as the capacity setpoint from the Nereda® Reactor via the FlowSPBlower variable, and sends the status of the blowers.                                                                                                 
                 Automation:  The aeration phase of each reactor sends a START/STOP command and an air capacity setpoint to the blower section. Each reactor will demand a maximum of two blowers.The number of requested blowers (1 or 2) is determined by the capacity setpoint from the Nereda® Reactor. The setpoint from the Nereda® Reactor is in the range of 0 to 100%. The blower capacity has to be scaled to the requested aeration capacity from the Nereda® Reactor.

  • 0% demand from the Nereda® Reactor means 0 Nm³/h.

  • 100% demand from the Nereda® Controller means two blowers at maximum capacity.

  • Blower capacity < C_MAX => 1 blower operating at the capacity calculated based on the capacity coming from the Nereda® Reactor (114 Nm³/h = max air capacity for 1 blower).

  • Blower capacity > C_MAX => 2 blowers operating at the capacity calculated based on the capacity coming from the Nereda® Reactor (197 Nm³/h = max air demand reactor).

Based on the start command from the reactor and the capacity from the Nereda® Reactor, the AerationNVPP logic will manage the two blowers as described below:

  • Single Blower Operation: If the capacity from the Nereda® Reactor is lower than C_MAX (the Blowers Running Setpoint) configured in the aeration settings on the HMI, only one blower will operate at the calculated capacity based on the capacity received from the reactor.

  • Dual Blower Operation: If the capacity from the Nereda® Reactor exceeds C_MAX (the Blowers Running Setpoint) configured in the aeration settings on the HMI, both blowers will initially start at a capacity of C2 (Starting Blowers Setpoint) or ramp down the running blower to an adjustable capacity of C2 to synchronize their operation. Afterward, the blowers will operate at the calculated capacity based on the capacity received from the Nereda® Reactor.

  • Transition from Two Blowers to One Blower: If both blowers are operating and the capacity from the Nereda® Controller drops below C_MAX (the Blowers Running Setpoint), the speed of both blowers will reduce to a setpoint of C3 (Below Capacity Setpoint), as defined in the aeration settings on the HMI. then, the system will switch to one blower operating at a speed calculated based on the capacity received from the Nereda® Reactor.
